# OKULAR business thesis — working hypotheses

**Job:** when asked to send money, data, credentials or access, help check the situation and find a safer independent next step.

**Products:** OKULAR Personal B2C (adoption and learning) and future OKULAR Platform B2B/B2B2C (API/SDK integrated by marketplaces, fintechs, housing, employment and other providers). Both share situation-aware safety engine; neither has real customers yet.

**Wedge candidate:** apartment/rental advance payments; identity impersonation is a demonstration second context. Validate with real interviews and independent source coverage.

**Model:** no revenue, customers, commitments, CAC, conversion, ROI or market share claimed. Pricing requires buyer interviews. COGS includes model inference, verifier costs, hosting, support, reviews, incident handling and legal costs; token bill is not total cost.

**Success signals:** better decisions in controlled testing, correct abstention and source traceability, time to safe next step, acceptable false-alarm rate and partner pilot interest with commitment. Do not equate a stopped transaction with fraud prevented.

**Governance:** privacy by default, no hidden monitoring, consent to analyze, jurisdiction-specific advice, correction/appeal mechanisms for institutional use.
